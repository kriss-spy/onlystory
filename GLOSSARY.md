# Onlystory

A private reading library for stories from multiple games.

## Language

**Event**:
A named collection of stories within a game. Main-story volumes and other named
collections occupy the same level of the library.
_Avoid_: Episode when referring to the whole collection.

**Story**:
An individually readable chapter or section within an event.
_Avoid_: Event when referring to a single chapter.

**Main route**:
The ordered stories that form an event's central narrative.

**Side-story branch**:
An optional story or sequence with its own reading order, separate from the main
route. A branch may be placed after a main story or remain outside its timeline.

**Passage**:
A single message or other reading step within a story.
_Avoid_: Story when referring to one message.
