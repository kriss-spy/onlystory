# Get started with Blue Archive

Use the [desktop release](https://github.com/kriss-spy/onlystory/releases/latest) for direct downloads. Story files are downloaded separately from the app and prepared on your device. No game account is needed.

[Watch the demo on YouTube](https://youtu.be/5lmKZqsqZWI) for a walkthrough of downloading the first Abydos collection and opening its first story.

## Choose a collection

1. Open Onlystory and choose **Blue Archive**.
2. Set the category to **Main story** and the sort order to **Source order**.
3. Start with the **Prologue** if you are new to the story. The first Abydos chapter is the collection immediately after it.

Each collection contains several individually readable stories. School filters and search help you find other collections. Event stories can spoil later main stories, so choose stories you have reached in your own game.

## Download a whole collection

1. In the catalog, press the **download button** in the top bar to enter selection mode.
2. Tick the checkbox beside the collection you want. This selects all its downloadable stories without opening the collection. You can select more than one collection.
3. Press the download button again. Its badge shows how many stories you selected.
4. Open **Download history**, the clock button beside the download button, to follow downloading and preparation. Keep Onlystory open until the stories show **Downloaded**.
5. Open the collection and choose its first story to read.

To download just one story, open its collection, choose the story and use **Download and import**. You can also select individual stories with the top-bar download button inside a collection.

The first download can be much larger than the installed story. Several stories depend on the same original asset packs and story databases. Onlystory downloads, checks and converts these files locally, then reuses shared files for later stories. Preparation can continue after the network transfer finishes.

You can browse other collections while downloads run. If you quit before a queue finishes, select the unfinished stories again next time; complete verified files can be reused. Once a story is installed, you can reopen it offline.

## Choose your language

Use the **Reading language** selector at the bottom of the reader to switch between Japanese and English where available. It keeps your current passage. Japanese voices remain Japanese when you choose English text.

The **EN** badge on a collection or story indicates verified official global English text. A collection can have English for only some of its stories. Stories without matched English retain Japanese dialogue and titles; this does not mean the download failed.

These settings are independent:

| Setting          | Where               | Changes                                              |
| ---------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Reading language | Reader toolbar      | Story dialogue and choices                           |
| App language     | Settings → Language | App menus, buttons and help                          |
| Catalog language | Settings → Language | Collection and story titles where translations exist |

The catalog follows an archived Japanese release snapshot. Its order and English coverage do not tell you whether a story has reached your global server.

## Set up the reader

Open **Settings** from the BA catalog, or **Reader settings** while reading. Appearance settings made here apply to Blue Archive.

For the look used in the demo, open **Appearance** and choose:

- **Appearance:** Dark.
- **Game theme:** BA.
- **Dialogue opacity:** 20%.

Under **Reading**, adjust dialogue size, text speed and auto reading delay. Under **Audio**, adjust voice, music and sound-effect levels. Preferences save on this device.

## Read and move around

- Click or tap the scene, press **Space**, or use **Next passage** to advance. Choose a response when one appears.
- Use the **progress bar** above the toolbar to seek along your selected story route. Dots mark choice points. Navigation past an unanswered choice stays locked until you answer it.
- Open **Chapters** to move between stories in the collection.
- Open **History** to search dialogue and return to a passage.
- Use **Bookmark passage** to mark a passage, or **Save points** to keep a named reading state you can restore later. Your current progress also saves automatically.
- Use **Auto reading** for automatic advance, **Fast-forward** to move quickly, and **Hide controls** or **Maximize reader** for more scene space.

Open **Commands & shortcuts** for the available keyboard controls. Common shortcuts include **A** for auto reading, **F** for fast-forward, **B** for a bookmark and **Ctrl+H** for history.

Some original native 3D cutscenes cannot play in the reader. When a notice offers **Continue story**, use it to move to the next supported passage.

## Using the browser version

BA in the browser requires you to download the listed original files yourself, then select those files or their folder for import. The desktop app fetches them directly. Both prepare stories locally, but their downloaded libraries are separate.

Follow [web setup and BA file imports](standalone-app.md#using-the-web-app) for the browser workflow. For development tools and local-library preparation, see the [asset setup guide](asset-setup-for-agents.md#blue-archive).
